FAQ

  • How do I unlock new levels? — Earn stars by completing levels. Some levels require a minimum number of stars to unlock.
  • How do I earn coins? — Collect orbs during runs. Use coins in the store to buy power-ups, skins, and accessories.
  • Are there any in-app purchases? — No. Parkour Runner is a one-time paid app with no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases. Everything is earned through gameplay.
  • The game is crashing. What should I do? — Make sure you're running the latest version of Parkour Runner and your iOS device. If the issue persists, contact us below with a short description of what you were doing when it crashed.
  • Does Parkour Runner work offline? — Yes. The game runs entirely on your device and does not require an internet connection.
  • How do I request a refund? — Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com within 90 days of your purchase.
