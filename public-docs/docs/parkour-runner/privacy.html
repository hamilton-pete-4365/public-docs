Parkour Runner

Privacy Policy

Overview

Parkour Runner is a single-player game that respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data Controller

Parkour Runner is developed by Pete Hamilton, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom. For the purposes of UK and EU data protection law, Pete Hamilton is the data controller. Contact: pete@curatedcfo.com.

Data Collection

Parkour Runner does not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, login, or any form of registration. All game progress — including stars, coins, unlocked items, and settings — is stored locally on your device.

Analytics & Tracking

We do not use any analytics services, tracking tools, or advertising frameworks. No data is sent to external servers.

Third-Party Services

Parkour Runner does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data.

Purchase Information

Parkour Runner is a paid app with a one-time upfront purchase. It contains no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no ads. Your purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using your Apple ID. We never see, receive, or store your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any data, the app is safe for users of all ages. Parkour Runner does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your Rights (UK and EU)

Under the UK GDPR, UK Data Protection Act 2018, and EU GDPR, you have rights including access to, correction of, and deletion of personal data held about you. Because Parkour Runner does not hold any personal data, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local EU supervisory authority.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date below.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please email pete@curatedcfo.com.
